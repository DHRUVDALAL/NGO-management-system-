Our Key Programs & Initiatives

Discover our core social programs designed to address educational inequalities, healthcare access, and youth development.

Education & Literacy

Gyan Setu: Evening Remedial Learning

A community-driven tutoring initiative targeting primary and secondary students from municipal schools. Volunteer teachers provide daily guidance in basic mathematics, science, and English to prevent dropouts and boost academic performance.

🎯 Beneficiaries: 350+ Underprivileged Students
📍 Locations: 4 Community Centers & Ward Schools
Sponsor a Student Teach as Volunteer
Healthcare & Hygiene

Arogya Sparsh: Mobile Health & Eye Camps

Bringing certified doctors, optometrists, and medical volunteers directly into informal settlements. We organize comprehensive vision tests, dispense prescription eyeglasses free of charge, and conduct preventative screenings for chronic ailments.

🎯 Beneficiaries: 1,200+ Screenings Annually
📍 Frequency: Twice Monthly Camps
Fund Eye Kits Join Medical Team
Youth Livelihood

Yuva Disha: Vocational & Digital Skills

Bridging the employment gap for high-school graduates and young adults through intensive workshops in computer basics, digital documentation, conversational English, and interview preparation.

🎯 Beneficiaries: 180+ Youths Certified
📍 Venue: Digital Learning Lab
CSR Partnership Mentor Youths
Emergency Relief

Poshak Aahar: Nutrition & Winter Relief

Distributing wholesome grocery rations and warm clothing to elderly residents, destitute families, and street workers during harsh winter months and monsoon flood emergencies.

🎯 Beneficiaries: 850+ Families Supported
📍 Scope: Emergency Response Drives
